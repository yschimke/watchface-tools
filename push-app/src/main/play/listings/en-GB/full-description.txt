Faces installs watch faces designed on the web onto your Wear OS watch, using Wear OS 6's Watch Face Push.

• Link your watch by scanning a QR code or entering a short code on a signed-in phone or computer.
• Browse the designs you have published and see a preview of each one.
• Install a design with one tap. Installing the same design again updates it.
• Make the new face your active watch face straight from the app.

Wear OS 6 gives each marketplace app one watch face slot, so installing a different design replaces the one Faces installed before. The app says so and asks you to confirm.

Every watch face is checked by Google's Watch Face Push validator before it can be installed.

Requires Wear OS 6 (API 36) or newer.
